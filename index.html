Questions

Good to know.

What photos work best?

Clear phone photos where faces are visible. One of each person and one together is perfect. Old photos work too.

Can the lines be in Hindi, Hinglish or another language?

Yes — English, Hindi, Hinglish, and more. For Tamil, Bengali, Marathi and others we have a native speaker check the lines.

Will it fit WhatsApp and Instagram?

Yes. You get a full-quality reel for Instagram and a lighter one that sends fast on WhatsApp.

Can I make one for a birthday or anniversary instead?

Yes — message us with the occasion. Love stories and anniversaries are our favourite.
