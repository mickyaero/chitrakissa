Questions

  
Which photos work best?
Clear phone photos where faces are visible: one of each person and one of you all together. Old photos work too.

  
Can the lines be in my language?
Hindi, Hinglish and English are ready today. For Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati or Punjabi, we have the lines checked by a native speaker before we send, which can take a little longer.

  
Will it fit WhatsApp and Instagram?
Yes. You get a full-quality film for Instagram and a lighter one that sends quickly on WhatsApp.

  
How do I get the full-quality film?
After you pay, by a private download link or as a file on Telegram. The link is deleted after 30 days.

  
Instagram makes my photos blurry. What should I do?
Send them on Telegram to @chitrakissa using "Send as file" for the sharpest result.

  
Can I order for a birthday or anniversary?
Yes, tell us the occasion when you message us. Love stories are our favourite.
