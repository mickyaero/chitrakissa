Questions

Which photos work best?

Clear phone photos where faces are visible. One of each person and one of you all together is perfect. Old photos work too.

Can the lines be in Hindi, Hinglish or my language?

Yes. Hindi, Hinglish and English are ready today. For other languages we check the lines with a native speaker, which can take a little longer.

Will it fit WhatsApp and Instagram?

Yes. You get a full-quality film for Instagram and a lighter one that sends quickly on WhatsApp.

How do I get the full-quality film?

The preview comes in your chat. After you pay, you get the full-HD film by a private download link (or as a file on Telegram). The link is deleted after 30 days.

Instagram makes my photos blurry. What should I do?

Instagram compresses photos a little, which is usually fine. For the sharpest result, send them on Telegram to @chitrakissa using "Send as file".

Can I order for a birthday or anniversary instead?

Yes. Tell us the occasion when you message us. Love stories and anniversaries are our favourite.
